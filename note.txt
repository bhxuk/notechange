Chapter One

The keeper of Gull Point

At dusk the light came on above the harbour, the way it had every night for ninety years. Mara watched it from the kitchen window with a mug going cold in her hands.

Her grandfather had kept the lamp until the day it was automated. After that he kept the logbook anyway, writing down the weather and the boats that passed, as if someone might one day ask.

Nobody ever did. But when the box of logbooks arrived at her door with a note in his careful handwriting, she sat down on the floor of the hallway and began to read.
