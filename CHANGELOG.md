# What's new

## 1.0.1

- **Fixed: Add this note did nothing on some iCloud accounts.** NoteChange now looks through the whole iCloud page, including the parts tucked inside frames and hidden sections, to find the open note.
- **New fallback.** If the note still can't be found, click inside it, press Ctrl+A to select it, and press Add this note. NoteChange reads what you selected.
- **Copy details.** When a note can't be read, a Copy details button describes how the page is built (never your note's text) so the problem can be fixed quickly.

## 1.0.0

NoteChange has been rebuilt from the ground up as a native Windows app.

- **Faster and smaller.** A proper Windows app that starts quickly, with a one-click installer and one-click updates.
- **New look.** Your collection on the left, a live preview in the middle, style and export on the right.
- **Better iCloud reading.** Notes are read from Apple's own iCloud page, including notes shown inside frames. Emphasis shown with styles, checklists and pictures in other formats (converted to PNG) now come across.
- **Real Word documents.** Proper Title and Heading styles, numbered and bulleted lists that restart correctly, nested lists, tables, links and pictures.
- **Better PDFs.** A4 pages with each note in a collection starting on a new page.
- **Markdown upgrades.** Checklists become task boxes and tables become Markdown tables.
- **Lists from plain text.** Pasted lines starting with `-`, `•`, `1.` or `[ ]` become real lists.
- **More ways in.** Open `.txt`, `.md` and `.html` files, or drag them onto the window. Collections saved by earlier versions still open.
- **Remembers your choices.** Style, title and text size are kept between sessions.
- **Sign out of iCloud** removes your Apple sign-in from this PC, and uninstalling NoteChange removes it too.
