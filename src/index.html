Ready when you are.

No rewriting. No account with us. No notes uploaded by NoteChange.

Three easy steps

  1. Press Open Apple Notes. Sign in to Apple and open the note you want. Press Use this note at the top.
  2. Check the preview. Choose whether the first line should be the title.
  3. Press Save Word document, choose where to save it, and press Save.

For email, Copy for email keeps formatting. Open a new email and paste. The subject is left for you to fill in.

Apple may ask you to approve sign-in on your iPhone. Notes need to be stored in iCloud. Locked notes and attachments may need to be opened or saved in Apple Notes first.

If a note cannot be read, copy it in Apple Notes, return here and press Paste my note.
