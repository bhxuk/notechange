Welcome to NoteChange

  1. Choose. Sign in to iCloud, select a note and press Add this note. Or paste a note.
  2. Preview. Choose Automatic, Recipe, Book chapter or Clean document. Check your words, pictures and any warning.
  3. Share. Save Word or PDF, or copy the formatted note into a new email.

Collections and chapters

Add several notes, move them into order and enable Export all added notes. Each note starts on a new page in Word or PDF. Save project keeps a reusable copy; Clear collection removes notes from this workspace.

Your privacy

Sign-in happens on Apple's page. NoteChange does not collect your password or send notes to a conversion service. Projects are saved only when you choose Save project. Notes in the current collection disappear when the app closes. Apple sign-in cookies are kept locally until you sign out.

Notes must be stored in iCloud. Apple may ask for iPhone approval. Locked notes, attachments and changes to Apple's web page can affect transfer. This public preview still needs real-account acceptance testing.

The installer is unsigned. Updates require a published GitHub release. For long or formatted email, use Copy for email and paste into a new message.
