# NoteChange: a quick guide

## First time

Open the NoteChange installer once. Follow any Windows prompt only if you trust the downloaded file. The installer puts **NoteChange** on your desktop.

## Save a note as a Word document

1. Double-click **NoteChange** on the desktop.
2. Press **Open Apple Notes**.
3. Sign in to Apple. Apple may ask you to approve this on your iPhone.
4. Open the note you want. Press **Use this note** at the top.
5. Check the preview. Choose whether the first line should be the title.
6. Press **Save Word document**. Choose a folder, then Save.

Your Apple note stays as it was.

## Put a note in an email

Press **Copy for email**, open a new email, then paste into the large message area. The title and note go in the message. Leave the subject blank or fill it in yourself. Add the recipient and check before sending. NoteChange never sends an email for you.

**Open email** starts a plain text email for shorter notes. If that doesn't open, use Copy for email. **More save options → Email draft** creates a file, but some email apps open it as a message rather than an editable draft.

## If the note won't appear

Open the note in Apple Notes, copy the content, return to NoteChange and press **Paste my note**. Or paste into the text box and press **Preview this text**.

Notes need to be saved in iCloud. If you cannot see a note at iCloud.com, check the iPhone Notes account/folder. Pictures and attachments may need separate saving; read any warning above the preview.

## Updates

When **Install update** appears, finish your current document and save it first. Press the button to restart NoteChange with the downloaded update.
